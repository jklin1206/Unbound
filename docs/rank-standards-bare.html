UNBOUND - standards by REAL cluster (corrected) + fixes

Grouped by the app's actual cluster field (not the id prefix) - so front & back levers, German hang, skin-the-cat, 360 pulls now sit under Pull; crow/crane/flying-crow/elbow-lever under Planche; plank & L-sit under Core; one-arm-handstand inside Handstand. Cells = bar to own that rank on that movement (reps · s · +N%×1 weighted 1-rep · ×N load). ✦=proposed mythic, ★=edge changed by the expert overhaul.

Fixes in this pass:   (1) Correct clusters - 16 nodes re-filed (levers→Pull, crow/elbow-lever→Planche, plank/L-sit→Core).   (2) Root-open bugs fixed - L-Sit Chin-Up now needs Chin-Up + L-Sit; Tuck Planche Push-Up now needs Tuck Planche.   (3) Weighted = 1-rep standard shown explicitly (weighted pull-up/chin-up = +N% ×1; weighted legs show their rep target).   (4) Mythic redefined to the endgame of each line: Full Back Lever, Full Front Lever, Full One-Arm Handstand, Full Planche, Muscle-Up, One-Arm Chin-Up, One-Arm Handstand, One-Arm Pull-Up, Press to Handstand, Strict Muscle-Up.   (5) 28 feat skills re-laddered ▲ - ranks are now the real exercise only: Initiate = 1 rep / 1s of the actual movement, scaled to an insane Unbound (full front lever 1s→40s, one-arm pull-up 1→12, full planche 1s→18s). No regressions, no borrowed exercises, no blanks - the drills/regressions to get there live in the unlock gate + program, not in the rank.   (6) Strict Muscle-Up no longer requires rings - re-parented onto the bar Muscle-Up.
